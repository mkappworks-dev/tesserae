# Terra audio credits

Every recording below is CC0 1.0. Effects are mono; ambience and music are
stereo, all 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis. Local ffmpeg decodes, resamples and trims;
oggenc encodes Vorbis because the installed ffmpeg lacks libvorbis. Full music
tracks have gentle end fades. Ambient beds use overlapping loop seams. Loudness
targets below describe authoring before the Game and player gains; they are not
a promise of output loudness or listening safety.

Bird calls are reserved for future nearby animal entities. Terra no longer
declares the old bird-and-synth music track; its source asset is retained for
reference. The green Biome uses environmental wind/rustle rather than
bird-and-synth ambience.

Material footsteps and rock breaks have an 8 ms attack fade and up to 60 ms
release, high-pass at 90 Hz, and peak matching to -8 dBFS before fades and Game
gains. Low-pass cutoffs: stone steps 5 kHz, grass/sand 3.5 kHz, shoe steps 4
kHz, rock breaks 4.2 kHz. Six stone and six grass/sand takes reduce
repeated-sample fatigue; wood and metal currently share four generic shoe takes.
Landings use the corresponding footstep family.
