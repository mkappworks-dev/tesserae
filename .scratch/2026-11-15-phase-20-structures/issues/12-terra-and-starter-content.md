# 12: Terra and the starter ship Templates

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: 03, 04, 05, 06, 07

Spec: [Phase 20](../spec.md).

## What to build

Terra declares a cabin, a well and a watchtower with a cellar, from Materials
it already has, and lists them in its Biomes at low chance. The SDK starter
declares one Template as the documented example. The Engine ships none.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Terra World shows each Template, level, whole and with its cellar
      underground.
- [ ] The starter's Template loads in its test.
- [ ] A World with no Game has no Template.
- [ ] Existing trees are unchanged.
