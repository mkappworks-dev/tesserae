# 11: The Client lists, turns and shows a Structure

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: 01, 10

Spec: [Phase 20](../spec.md).

## What to build

Holding the `place` Tool, a Creative Player opens the Template list with Use,
turns the Structure with `R`, sees the ghost tinted per cell and places with
Place, with the HUD and refusals approved in ticket 01.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Client matches the approved mockup.
- [ ] The list shows every Template the Game and its Mods declare, with none
      hard-coded.
- [ ] `R` turns by quarter turns and the ghost follows the aim.
- [ ] A refusal from the Server shows as the alert the other Tools use.
