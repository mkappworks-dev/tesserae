# 10: Wood in Survival

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: None (can start immediately)

Spec: [Phase 16](../spec.md), amendment 2026-11-14.

## What to build

A Survival player in a new Terra World finds single Wood Voxels on temperate
grass, every 6 m, breaks them for Wood, and crafts a Workbench, then a Chest and
a Door. Terra's temperate Biome gains a Wood surface feature beside its melons.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new Terra World has Wood on temperate grass near spawn; breaking one
      gives one Wood.
- [ ] A Terra test shows a Survival Player can gather enough Wood within a short
      walk of spawn for a Workbench, a Chest and a Door (15 Wood).
- [ ] Existing Terra pins (surface features, Biome tables) are updated.
