# 03: Terra's field, orchard, paddock and garden Templates

Status: ready-for-agent

Spec: [Settlement farms](../spec.md).

## What to build

In the mockup's builders, exported to Terra: wheat, carrot and potato fields and
a mixed one (farmland rows of a crop's first Stage, a lane, a low edge), an
orchard of apple, cherry or orange trees in rows, a fenced paddock with a gate,
and a fenced vegetable garden small enough for a disc Plot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every Template validates and every Material in it is Obtainable.
- [ ] Crops in a Template grow and are harvested like planted ones.
