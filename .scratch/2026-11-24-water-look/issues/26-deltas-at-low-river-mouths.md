# 26: Deltas at low river mouths

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 25

Spec: [Water that looks natural](../spec.md), Mouths. From the user's review:
add deltas near oceans.

## What to build

A river 4 m wide or more that meets the sea over a low coast fans into a
delta: branches that split again, each six to ten times its width long, with
the land between them lifted to just above the sea.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Most river mouths on low coasts have a delta; a map and captures shown
      to the user.
