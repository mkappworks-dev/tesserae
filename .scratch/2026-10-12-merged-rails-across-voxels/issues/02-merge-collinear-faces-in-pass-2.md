# Merge collinear faces across Voxels in pass 2

Status: done
Blocked by: 01

by the maintainer. Read the spec first: decisions 1 to 5 are the contract.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Pass 2 merges coplanar, abutting shaped quads of equal Material,
render layer and `Light` across Voxels, so a floor of slabs or stairs and a
fence line emit a fraction of the quads.

**Current behavior:**
Pass 2 emits each shaped Voxel's quads on its own and "nothing merges across
Voxels". A flat slab floor of 1680 quads, terrain with 1024 stairs 4368, and a
fence line of 12 about 226 (ticket 01's table is the reference).

**Desired behavior:**
After culling and lighting each quad as today, pass 2 merges quads that are on
the same face and plane, abut along a full shared edge at a Voxel boundary, and
agree on Material, render layer and flat `Light`. It never merges across a
Chunk boundary, never merges quads that differ in any of those, and never draws
anything the unmerged mesh did not (a culled area stays culled). UVs continue
across a merged run so a tiling sampler repeats per Voxel exactly as it did,
and a half-height side still shows the lower half of the texture. Vertex layout,
winding and the shader are unchanged.

**Key interfaces:**

- The shaped-Voxel pass of the mesher: it stops pushing quads straight to the
  layer, collects them as integer rectangles, merges, then pushes. The buffer
  may be reused between Chunks if that is cheaper; that is the implementation's
  to decide against the benchmark.
- `ChunkMesh`/`MeshLayer` and `ShapeQuad`: no change to their public shape.
- The doc comments that say "nothing merges across Voxels" (the pass-2 comment
  and anywhere else it is repeated) are brought in line.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] The hole-free property test passes over all its seeds unchanged: every
      exposed sub-cell face is drawn, none twice (its oracle reads each quad's
      own extent, so a merged quad is covered)
- [ ] Tests that count a fence line's quads are updated to the merged count
- [ ] New tests: a line of fences merges its arms; a floor of slabs and a floor
      of stairs merge their coplanar tops; nothing merges across a `Light`
      step, a Material change, a gap, or a Chunk boundary (a fence line that
      crosses one is two quads' worth, not one); merged and unmerged meshes cover
      the same sub-cell squares; a merged quad's UVs match the unmerged quads'
      at the corners they share
- [ ] Interleaved A/B against the commit this builds on, quiet machine (load
      under 5, medians within ~2% of minimums), four or more rounds: pass 1,
      the light solvers, `greedy_mesh/terrain`, `/solid`, `/checkerboard` and
      `mesh_chunk/straddling` do not move; `terrain_stairs_at_cap` and
      `all_stairs` add at most +25%; the numbers go in this file's Comments
- [ ] `terrain_fences_at_cap` and `flat_fences_at_cap` are reported with their
      quad counts before and after, and the cap constant's doc comment still
      holds (`cap_units` is not changed by this ticket)
- [ ] If the +25% gate cannot be met, say so with the numbers and set this
      ticket to `wontfix`; do not ship a pass 2 over the gate
- [ ] `./scripts/cargo-ci` passes; `npm run fmt` and `cargo fmt --all` are run

**Out of scope:**

- Changing `cap_units`, `MAX_SHAPED_PER_CHUNK` or the cap's rule (spec review
  question 3)
- Merging in pass 1, or across Chunks
- Merging quads that differ in `Light`, Material or render layer
- Changing the vertex layout, the shader, or how light is baked
- Changing which quads a Shape emits or how pass 2 culls them

## Comments

Done, inside the +25% gate. Pass 2 collects each surviving quad as a rectangle
over the Chunk (after culling and lighting, as before), sorts by plane, Material,
layer and light, merges abutting rows and then columns, and writes what is left.
A quad with no edge on a Voxel boundary skips the sort and is written at once.
The hole-free property test passes over all its seeds unchanged; the new tests
(`a_floor_of_slabs_...`, `a_line_of_fences_costs_the_same_...`, and those for a
light step, a change of Material, a gap, UVs and a Chunk's own extent) pass.

## Follow-up: cheaper merge, and the pass-1 residue explained

Three things left over from the first pass at this ticket, closed.

**The gallery capture after merging** was taken (`TESSERAE_SCREENSHOT_OFFSCREEN=1`,
fly 0). Two captures of the final build are byte-identical, and the fence line,
corner, stair, cubes and slabs read as before. Against the capture from before
the merge, 107 of 242,000 pixels in the gallery region differ (mean 3.8 of 255,
max 33), almost all on distant slope-smoothing terrain edges and none on a
missing face: edge-pixel rasterisation where quads now join, not geometry.

**The merge is cheaper.** Quads are counted into one bucket per face and plane
(6 x 513 buckets, one array) and the two sorts run per plane, not over the whole
Chunk. `merge_shaped` is kept out of line. Interleaved A/B, six rounds, load
2.5, medians; `base` is the commit before the merge (`76d28a3`):

The gate (`terrain_stairs_at_cap` and `all_stairs` at no more than +25%) holds
with room, and `mesh_chunk/straddling`, which holds 209 shaped Voxels and so
runs pass 2, is back to where it was. The first version of this change made it
+8% because the bucket table had a fixed cost; the sorts were not the cost on a
Chunk that small, the table and the emission were.

**Pass 1's residue was code layout, not work.** The branch's `terrain` read +1%
to +5% against `main` while pass 1's source is identical. A fixed-iteration
driver, run under `/usr/bin/time -l`, counts instructions retired and cycles for
`main` and the branch: instructions are 0.02% lower on the branch for terrain, a
full Chunk and an empty one, and cycles are equal (terrain 5.61e9 against
5.62e9) once `mesh_shaped` is left to be inlined as it is on `main` and only
`merge_shaped` is kept out of line. With `mesh_shaped` marked `inline(never)`
the same instructions took 3.8% more cycles on terrain, and with `pass1` marked
`inline(never)` 18% more: the hot loop is sensitive to what is inlined around it
and that, not extra work, was the residue. `checkerboard` earlier swinging
-4.5% to +6% was the same effect between builds. This is the number to read for
"does pass 1 move": it does not.
