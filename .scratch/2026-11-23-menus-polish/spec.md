# Menus polish

## Problem Statement

A side-by-side review of the Client against the approved mockup, after
[Menus that feel like a game](../2026-11-22-game-menus/spec.md), found places
where the two disagree, a few Client bugs, and some screens where neither is
right yet:

- The Map opens with the last Waypoint still selected.
- Edit World's Esc Cancel throws away unsaved edits without asking.
- The World step's Game mode and Voxel size do not match the mockup, and the
  step overflows so Voxel size and Climate scale are hidden.
- Mods without a thumbnail, including the Character Mods and Terra's required
  Mods, show colour strips instead of a picture.
- The trust modal lists every permission and limit and is taller than the
  window.
- The Biomes side panel and biome pictures do not match the mockup.
- Overlays dim the scenery less than the mockup does.
- The Inventory's search hides behind an icon, and the item popup in the
  Catalogue, Crafting and workstation panes is cut off at the pane's edge.
- The Layout-ready HUD card spells out every key in a sentence.
- The Worlds and Trust sheets fill the screen when their content is short.
- A filter, sort or other menu popup stays open when you click elsewhere.
- The Mods step list does not scroll, and the Biomes step still draws the rail
  as a separate box.

## Solution

The mockup stays the design: where it changes, it changes first and is approved
before the Client follows. The Client then matches it screen by screen, and the
bugs are fixed.

## User Stories

1. As a Player, I want the Map to open centred on me with nothing selected, so
   that its prompts start with what I can do there
2. As a Player, I want Esc on Edit World to ask before it discards my edits, so
   that I do not lose a rename by accident
3. As a Player, I want Game mode as one Creative | Survival control and Voxel
   size as one selector, so that the World step fits on one screen
4. As a Player, I want every Mod and Game to have a picture, so that I can tell
   them apart at a glance
5. As a Player, I want a Character Mod's picture to show the character, so that
   I know who I will play as
6. As a Player, I want the trust modal to show each item's name, build and
   whether it runs code, with a Details toggle for its permissions and limits,
   so that it fits on screen and I can still check what I agree to
7. As a Player, I want a compact biome panel and a diorama picture for each
   biome, with sliders for exact climate values, so that I can tell biomes apart
   and set them with any input
8. As a Player, I want overlays to dim the scenery as much as the mockup does,
   so that the menu reads clearly over a bright World
9. As a Player, I want the Inventory's search as a field under the tabs, so
   that I can see where to type
10. As a Player, I want an item's popup to show in full wherever I point, so
    that no detail is cut off
11. As a Player, I want the Layout-ready card to show L Edit, ⌫ Exit and RMB
    Place as key caps, so that it is short and still tells me how to place
12. As a Player, I want a sheet to be as tall as its content, up to the screen,
    so that short pages do not look empty
13. As a Player, I want the Mods step list to scroll, so that I can reach every
    Mod
14. As a Player, I want a click outside a popup to close it, so that popups
    behave as they do everywhere else

## Implementation Decisions

- The mockup changes first, in one ticket, and is approved before any Client
  ticket that depends on it: the trust modal's Details toggle, the Edit World
  discard dialog, the Biomes panel with Temperature and Humidity sliders and
  Caves, the Inventory search field under the tabs, the Layout-ready card with
  RMB Place, and a new Place Tool page with a side panel like Quick layout's.
  The Place Tool page is mockup-only; the Client does not implement it here.
- Clearing the Map's selection on open keeps the Map centred on the Player.
- The Edit World discard dialog opens only when there are unsaved edits, with
  Keep editing focused, so Enter never discards.
- The World step and Edit World drop the name hint, the per-mode descriptions
  and the seed echo; one line under Game mode describes the chosen mode.
- Thumbnails are generated by `scripts/generate-thumbnails` for every Game and
  Mod that has none. A Character Mod's thumbnail is its figure drawn from its
  own skin. Each new thumbnail changes that Mod's build hash, as ticket 03 of
  the game-menus spec accepted.
- The trust modal's Details list scrolls inside the modal, which never grows
  past the window. Cancel stays focused.
- A biome's picture is a small isometric diorama drawn from its own settings
  (surface, soil, rock, sky, fog) and redraws when they change. The sliders and
  Caves stay because a controller needs exact values.
- The darker dim applies to every overlay and dialog, including World details,
  whose content and title stay as they are.
- Sheet height is shared frame code: a sheet hugs its content up to the screen
  and scrolls inside past that. Settings is rechecked after the change.

## Testing Decisions

- Behaviour changes are tested the way the game-menus tests drive a screen
  (acts, keys, pointer; what is shown, focused and prompted): the Map's
  selection on open, the discard dialog, the trust Details toggle, the Mods step
  scrolling and the Inventory search.
- The thumbnail generator is checked by the existing thumbnail validation tests
  on its output (size, format, path), and new thumbnails ship in the demo Games
  and Mods.
- Visual-only changes (dim, sheet height, card layout, diorama) are checked by
  capture against the mockup; captures are not committed.
- Demo Game tests run on Terra only.

## Out of Scope

- The Place Tool in the Client.
- World details content (kept as it is).
- Trust in dev builds, which still trust the bundled Games.
