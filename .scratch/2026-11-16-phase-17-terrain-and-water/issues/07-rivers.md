# 07: Rivers, valleys and waterfalls (17b)

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: 06

Spec: [Phase 17](../spec.md).

## What to build

Flow accumulation to river segments of growing width, a valley profile carved by distance, deltas, and a stepped Water surface with waterfalls where the drop exceeds a Voxel.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every river reaches a lake or the sea, or is cut at the halo by definition.
- [ ] A river is at the same metres at every Voxel size.
- [ ] Phase 20's bridges find a river crossing with no change to their rule.
