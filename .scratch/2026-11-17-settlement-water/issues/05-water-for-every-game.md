# 05: Ponds and waterfalls for every demo Game

Status: done

Blocked by: 04

Spec: [Water by Settlements](../spec.md).

## What to build

Meadow, Lumen, Tidewater and Ember each declare a pond, a lily or reed pond, a
brook-fed pond and a waterfall in their own Materials, and their hamlets and
villages lay them as a water group by chance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every new Material is Obtainable in its Game.
- [x] Each Game's Settlements lay water near spawn somewhere in a scan.
