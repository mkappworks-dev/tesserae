# 16: Vegetation across every Biome and Game

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: 12, 14

Spec: [Phase 18](../spec.md), amendment 2026-11-14.

## What to build

The maintainer wants every Biome of every in-repo Game to look alive, with many
kinds of tree and plant. All of it is data on the Plant contract, except vines in
worldgen, which needs one engine addition.

- Tree species as `tree` Plants, each with its own grown trunk and canopy
  Materials (placed look-alikes for building), size in metres, saplings and
  yields: oak, birch, pine, palm and at least two fruit trees (apple, orange),
  and a berry or nut tree if a Game has room for one. Pine suits cold Biomes,
  palm dry and coastal ones, birch cool ones, fruit trees temperate and warm.
- Vegetables and fruit as `crop`s that Biomes scatter wild (carrot, potato,
  berries) beside wheat, with food Items.
- Ground cover, flowers, mushrooms, cane, kelp and cacti where they fit each
  Biome.
- Vines: climbers that worldgen hangs from the outer canopy of tree species that
  declare them (jungle and swamp trees), by a pure function of the tree so they
  are whole across Chunk borders, and that then grow by the existing `climber`
  rule. A `tree-def` gains an optional `vines` (a climber Plant name, a density
  from 0 to 0.5 and a length in metres). Vines are generated by worldgen and
  never by a growing sapling.
- Every in-repo Game (`terra`, `meadow`, `ember`, `tidewater`, `lumen`) gets
  vegetation suited to its Biomes through `flora` or its own rows, so no Game
  has a Biome of bare ground by accident. A Game with no Biomes or a deliberately
  bare World (a test fixture) is left alone.
- Wood still comes only from trees: no standalone Wood anywhere.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Terra generates, in the matching Biomes, oak, birch, pine, palm, apple and
      orange trees, wild vegetables and berries, flowers, mushrooms, cane, kelp
      and cacti, in a headless generation test that counts each by name.
- [ ] A fruit tree's fruit ripens, is picked with Use and regrows; every new
      fruit is a 3D box Shape as the approved mockup shows.
- [ ] A jungle or swamp tree generates vines hanging from its canopy, whole
      across Chunk borders and equal at 1.0, 0.5 and 0.25 m; a vine whose
      anchor is removed breaks by the support rule.
- [ ] `meadow`, `ember`, `tidewater` and `lumen` each generate vegetation that
      fits their Biomes, and their starter and load tests pass.
- [ ] No Biome of a shipped Game is bare unless it is declared so, and a
      generation test asserts it.
- [ ] Digests of Worlds that declare no Plant features are unchanged.
- [ ] `tesserae validate` passes for `flora` and every Game.
